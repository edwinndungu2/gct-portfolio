PLAN BEFORE YOU CONNECT

Find the cause of
the weak spots.

Connectivity depends on more than the router. Space layout, walls, device locations and the incoming internet connection can all affect performance.

Share where and when you experience problems. That context helps us discuss useful options without guessing at a one-size-fits-all fix.

  • Property size, floors and layout
  • Rooms or work areas with poor connection
  • Number and type of connected devices
  • Existing router and internet provider
  • Wired connections, Wi-Fi extension or setup needs
